Privacy policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

THorn HTTP does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What stays on your device

The rules, groups and settings you create are saved in your browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.local). Network recordings you start are kept in memory and are only saved if you export them yourself. None of this leaves your device.

What we don't do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or advertising.
  • No requests to our own or third-party servers.
  • We never sell or transfer data, because we don't have any.

Permissions

The extension needs access to the sites you browse only to apply the rules you create there. See the store listing for why each permission is requested.

Delay rules run entirely in your browser: nothing is sent to any server, ours included. Delaying scripts, styles, images and fonts uses Chrome's debugger only if you allow it; Chrome then shows a "started debugging this browser" bar while it's in use, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings.

This website

thorn-http.dev is a static site without cookies or analytics. Your browser still sends standard request information (such as your IP address) to the hosting provider when you visit it.
